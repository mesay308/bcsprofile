## 1. Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS) and Oracle Cloud

### 1.1 Brief Profile

Oracle is our lead platform for large and complex enterprises. BCS is an Oracle Partner Network member, authorized to sell, design, and implement Oracle technology, including Oracle Database, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), and Oracle's ERP, HCM, CRM, EPM, and SCM systems. Clients can also use Oracle's global support centers in Buenos Aires, Bucharest, Bangalore, and Beijing, which provide round-the-clock help in 24 languages.

Our Oracle work is built on experience in process manufacturing, public financial management (IFMIS), and banking. We bring the same audit trails, reconciliation controls, and ledger integrity used on core banking systems to every Oracle implementation.

## 2. Odoo Enterprise

### 2.1 Brief Profile

Odoo is business management software that originated in Belgium. BCS is an official Odoo partner. We deploy its 30 core applications, along with a global library of more than 16,000 community modules that can be adapted locally. Together they cover accounting, inventory, manufacturing, point of sale (POS), CRM, e-commerce, and project management on one integrated platform.

Odoo Enterprise gives mid-sized and growing Ethiopian enterprises the same advanced capabilities as a large ERP, at a lower cost of ownership and with faster rollout.
