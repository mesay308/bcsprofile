BCS Project Approach

BCS runs enterprise projects with a proven, business-driven approach. It is adapted from the Oracle Unified Methodology (OUM) for Business Flows and the PMI Project Management Standards. We do not treat implementation as a purely technical installation of software features. Instead, we build delivery around end-to-end Business Flows, bringing together People, Process, and Technology to drive measurable operational results.

7.1 Core Value Drivers

Speed, Value, and Simplicity

Pre-configured business process flows and standard templates shorten design cycles and speed up return on investment.

Process-First Alignment

Business requirements guide system configuration, so workflows match real operational needs and regulatory standards.

Continuous Stakeholder Engagement

Early hands-on checks through Conference Room Pilots (CRPs) reduce late rework and build ownership inside your team.

Milestone-Driven Accountability

Clear phase gates link each deliverable to a quality sign-off and a predictable schedule.

7.2 The Three Pillars of BCS Delivery ("The 3 A's")

Accelerators

Standard business process models, pre-built templates, and shared implementation toolkits that shorten delivery timelines.

Architecture

A unified, scalable data model built for high availability, smooth third-party integration, and well-tuned server and cloud infrastructure.

Adoption

Structured Organizational Change Management (OCM), role-based training, and knowledge transfer, so your teams can work on their own after go-live.

7.3 Deployment Strategy Options

We match the rollout sequence to your operational risk, resource capacity, and business priorities.

Phased / Wave Rollout (recommended for multi-departmental scopes)
How It Works

Functional areas go live in logical waves. For example, Wave 1 covers Core Finance, Procurement, and Inventory. Wave 2 covers HR, Payroll, Operations, and Advanced Analytics. This reduces disruption, brings early value, and lets teams apply lessons to later waves.

Best When

The scope covers many departments.

Big-Bang / Single-Wave Rollout
How It Works

All in-scope modules go live at the same time.

Best When

Departments depend so closely on each other that running the old system in parallel is not practical.

7.4 Project Management and Control Framework

To protect timelines, budgets, and quality against scope creep and operational risks, BCS pairs its implementation lifecycle with PMI-aligned project controls.

Scope and Change Control

Any change to agreed requirements after Phase 2 sign-off follows a formal Change Request (CR) process. It assesses the impact on time, cost, and architecture before the Steering Committee approves it.

Risk and Issue Management

We keep a live RAID Log (Risks, Assumptions, Issues, and Dependencies). Both Project Managers review it every week, so risks are handled early.

Quality Assurance

Every deliverable goes through internal BCS peer review before it is submitted to you and before formal acceptance testing.
